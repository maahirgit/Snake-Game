## Relationships

- A **Board** holds one or more **Snakes** and exactly one **Food** at a time
- A **Player** steers exactly one **Snake** and owns exactly one **Score**
- A **Snake** has one **Head**, one **Tail**, and a **Body** of three or more cells
- Eating a **Food** causes **Growth** and raises that player's **Score**
- Every fifth **Food Eaten** raises the **Level**, which shortens the **Tick**
- A **Collision** with a **Wall** or with a **Body** ends the game in **Game Over**

## Example dialogue

> **Dev:** "When a **Snake** eats the **Food**, does the **Score** rise before or after **Growth**?"

> **Domain expert:** "Same **Tick**. The **Snake** grows and the **Player**'s **Score** rises together, and a new **Food** appears somewhere no **Body** is."

> **Dev:** "So if two **Snakes** are on the **Board** and both **Heads** reach the **Food** on the same **Tick**?"

> **Domain expert:** "One of them eats it. That is a rule we have never had to write down, because there has only ever been one **Snake**."

> **Dev:** "And the **High Score** — is that all-time?"

> **Domain expert:** "No. It survives a **Game Over** but not quitting the program. It is the best of this sitting."

## Flagged ambiguities

- **"Food" names both the category and one of its two kinds.** `Food` is the base
  class *and* the ordinary ten-point item, with `SpecialFood` as the other kind
  (`src/Snake.cpp:29`, `src/Snake.cpp:62`). Recommendation: keep **Food** for the
  category and say **Ordinary Food** for the ten-point kind.
- **"Speed" means two opposite quantities.** `getSpeed()` returns a *delay* in
  milliseconds, so a larger number is slower (`src/Snake.cpp:420`), while
  `updateStats()` displays `200 - getSpeed()` under the label "Speed", where a larger
  number is faster (`src/Snake.cpp:321`). Recommendation: **Tick Delay** for the
  millisecond value and **Speed** for what the player is shown.
- **"Collision" covers eating.** The code treats the head reaching the food as a
  collision (`src/Snake.cpp:400`) alongside wall and self collisions
  (`src/Snake.cpp:387`, `src/Snake.cpp:394`), but only the latter two end the game.
  Recommendation: reserve **Collision** for the fatal cases and say **Eating** for food.
- **"High Score" implies permanence it does not have.** It is persisted to
  `highscore.txt` (`src/Snake.cpp:171`) but `clearHighScoreFile()` zeroes it on quit
  (`src/Snake.cpp:722`, `README.md:73`). Recommendation: **Session Best**.
